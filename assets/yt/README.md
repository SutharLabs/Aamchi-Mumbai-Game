## Device crops

YouTube recommends a 2560 × 1440 px banner up to 6 MB. It specifies a 1235 × 338 px text and logo safe area on the minimum 2048 × 1152 px canvas. For this banner, that guide is scaled proportionally and rounded inward to **1542 × 422 px**. The centered guide starts at (509, 509) and ends just before (2051, 931). [YouTube channel branding guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en)

- **Mobile and narrow views** keep the rickshaw mark, both title lines, and the channel description inside the center guide.
- **Desktop** shows more of the lane along a wide center strip. Available width varies by device.
- **TV** uses the full canvas, with the street artwork extending to every edge.
- **Profile picture** was checked with a circular mask at YouTube’s 98 × 98 px display size. The gold M, rickshaw, and road markings fit inside the circle.

The encoded JPG was checked at full size, as a simulated desktop strip, and as the narrow safe-area crop. In YouTube Studio, open **Customization → Profile** to upload both images and check the device previews before publishing.
