# Ranwar vehicle audio sources

The engine loops use short edits of genuine engine recordings from the creators' high-quality public Freesound previews. Each edit was band-filtered, converted to 24 kHz mono signed 16-bit PCM, level-matched, and joined with a phase-matched overlap at the loop seam. The trims below identify the source ranges before loop overlap and final length adjustment. Every recording is offered by its source under [Creative Commons Zero 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| Game files | Recording and source range | Creator and source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hatchback-idle.wav` | Citroën C4 stationary engine, 1.00–4.08 s | [lattes3, Citroen C4 Engine](https://freesound.org/people/lattes3/sounds/767500/) |
| `hatchback-loaded.wav` | Unspecified car holding an elevated engine speed, 0.07–1.55 s | [FreeCarSoundsGaming, Revving_2.ogg](https://freesound.org/people/FreeCarSoundsGaming/sounds/535040/) |
| `rickshaw-idle.wav`, `rickshaw-loaded.wav` | Mumbai auto-rickshaw interior, 20.00–21.55 s and 211.25–213.65 s | [ambar das, Inside an Auto Rickshaw in Mumbai](https://freesound.org/people/ambar%20das/sounds/203177/) |
| `motorcycle-idle.wav` | Unspecified motorcycle at idle, 4.25–7.45 s | [lmbubec, Motorbike Idling.wav](https://freesound.org/people/lmbubec/sounds/119455/) |
| `motorcycle-loaded.wav` | Honda CRM 250 onboard engine, 24.65–26.36 s | [martian, motorbike crm 250 cc onboard ride pullaway.wav](https://freesound.org/people/martian/sounds/148868/) |
| `scooter-idle.wav`, `scooter-loaded.wav` | Automatic scooter recorded in Jakarta, 36.64–39.19 s and 103.83–105.62 s | [Kang_Alpin, Automatic Scooter Engine](https://freesound.org/people/Kang_Alpin/sounds/676835/) |

The recordings are acoustic references for the fictional game vehicles. Only the rickshaw recording was made in Mumbai. `RanwarAudio` crossfades each idle and loaded pair and varies playback speed with throttle and road speed. The horns and footsteps are synthesized by the game.
