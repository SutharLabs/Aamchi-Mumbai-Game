# Bandra Channel bridge source

The original Blender generator models the Bandra Channel segment of the Bandra–Worli Sea Link. It covers 600 m between expansion joints, with two 250 m cable-stayed main spans and two 50 m transition spans. Each of its independent carriageways has four 3.5 m lanes inside the published 14 m carriageway width and a 126 m pylon measured from the pile-cap datum. Two fish-belly girders, tapered four-leg pylons, semiharp cables, guardrails, anchors and lamp poles provide the bridge's characteristic profile. [MSRDC project facts](https://msrdc.in/Site/Common/ProjectListDetails.aspx?ID=66&MainId=18) · [MSRDC operating concession, schedule A](https://msrdc.in/Site/Upload/GR/3_RGSL%20RFP%20Vol%20III%20-%20Schedule%20-18-10-2013%20R8.pdf) · [Construction engineering paper](https://doi.org/10.2749/222137813808627587)

The game model approximates the beam and anchor sections, deck aprons, lamp positions, the pylon profiles and the number and diameter of the modeled cables. The asphalt uses a flat local Z=0 reference. The ocean in the inspection views sits 24.8 m below the road, which gives the published 20 m clearance below the midpoint girder. The pilot segment stops at the expansion joints. Scene-level coastline and connecting approaches belong to the district expansion. The generator constructs all geometry and surface images. The contractor and formwork provider's photos served as visual references for proportions. [HCC completed bridge gallery](https://www.hccindia.com/products/bridges-and-elevated-roads) · [Doka formwork reference](https://www.doka.com/en/references/asia/bandraworlisealink)

The FBX and GLB contain only the bridge. Before creating the inspection stills, the Blender script reimports both exports and compares their bounds with the source geometry. The asset spans X from −300 to +300 m, Y from −22.1 to +22.1 m and Z from −29.8 to +100 m. It contains eight mesh groups, seven material slots, 29,224 source triangles and two original 512 × 512 sRGB colour tiles. Four-metre tile UVs are in both formats. The FBX carries 100 cm per source metre, and the GLB carries metre-scale mesh coordinates. The PNGs show the same bridge from above and at road level using Blender's Cycles CPU renderer.
