Aamchi Mumbai Game adapted Neutral_ID.bvh, Neutral_FW.bvh and Neutral_FR.bvh.
The adaptations extract and trim loops, smooth the loop seams, normalize the
heading, remove horizontal root translation, retarget to the game skeleton and
resample the tracks for the in-game idle, walk and jog.
