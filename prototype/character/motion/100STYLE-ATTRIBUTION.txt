Aamchi Mumbai Game adapted Neutral_ID.bvh, Neutral_FW.bvh, Neutral_FR.bvh
and TwoFootJump_FW.bvh.
The adaptations extract and trim loops, smooth the loop seams, normalize the
heading, remove horizontal root translation, retarget to the game skeleton and
resample the tracks for idle, walk, jog, and jump takeoff, air and landing.
The jump adaptation removes its recorded travel and aligns the sole height so
the gameplay controller supplies the actual movement.
