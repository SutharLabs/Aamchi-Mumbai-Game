## Sharing an asset between Godot and Unreal

Both engines can ingest the same conventional GLB mesh or skeleton, PNG texture and PCM WAV when the asset's terms allow it. Prefer a Blender or generator source plus a versioned exchange, and maintain one copy of the authored data. The Ranwar road handoff uses the existing geometry JSON and Blender FBX-with-textures pipeline. Inputs shared between projects keep their original units, stable identifiers and source receipt. The importer handles the Godot-to-Unreal axis and metre-to-centimetre conversions. [Godot formats](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/about/list_of_features.html) · [Unreal glTF support](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/gltf-file-format-support-in-unreal-engine) · [Ranwar exchange](../unreal/ContentSource/RANWAR_STREET.md)

Each engine owns its material graph, collision and navigation setup, animation retargeting, input, UI, save data and gameplay code. The pickup demonstrates this separation. Godot uses an AudioStreamPlayer3D. Unreal creates a spatial one-shot from the packaged PCM. Each plays at the pickup point after the mission checkpoint saves successfully.
