# GTA gameplay reference and Ranwar implementation

Desk review on **22 November 2026**. GTA V Story Mode and GTA Online serve different purposes. In Story Mode, missions can become available through character-specific progress, time, location and phone contacts. GTA Online uses cash for purchases and reputation for unlocks, with some one-time completion bonuses alongside its repeatable jobs. Ranwar uses a persistent story contact and an Indian rupee payout tied to one completed job. [Rockstar Story Mode mission help](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/2anfBi4NMr3QqtUMeum51b/what-to-do-if-no-further-missions-are-showing-up-in-gtav-story-mode) · [Rockstar Online starter tips](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o349k5525534k7/gta-online-starter-tips) · [Rockstar one-time rewards](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/1knqB39UwZUkFUNwTQktbh/how-to-track-special-heist-completion-bonuses-in-gta-online)

The official GTA V game help documents separate health and money readouts, revival at a hospital with a charge, and a police response with five wanted levels. It also describes character-specific abilities and experience-based stats. The values below are original Ranwar playtest settings. [GTA V game help on Xbox](https://dlassets-ssl.xboxlive.com/public/content/4f0a3089-ba2c-4f3d-9e38-102a41cbd885/GameManual/bffef18e-3f19-4190-a9b1-75402359b13b/en-SA/index.html)

| System | Ranwar playtest |
| --- | --- |
| Payout and story | Deliver the parcel for ₹500 and unlock a conversation with Mira on Hill Road. The conversation adds the ledger lead to the paused status. The completion receipt is the source of the payout, including for profiles that completed the parcel before this update. |
| Spending | Use first aid by the vehicle bays for ₹60 when injured and clear of a police alert. Clinic recovery costs up to ₹100 and detention up to ₹150. The versioned local profile tracks spending and the story lead. |
| Injury and recovery | Faster frontal impacts can injure a driver or a pedestrian player. Health starts at 100 and recovers gradually toward 50 after seven quiet seconds when below half and clear of an alert. At zero health, the player reappears on foot at the recovery point. Active jobs can retry the last objective from the board. |
| Police | Significant impacts into a pedestrian or running traffic can trigger up to two alert levels and one marked, lane-following patrol. It checks the area around the player, slows nearby and can detain a stopped player it can see. An alert clears after sufficient time out of sight. Using Recover during an alert invokes detention. |
| Visibility and saves | The HUD shows injury or police status while relevant. Pause shows health, cash, and the logged story lead. Health and police state are per profile for the running game session and survive a return to the title. A full application restart starts a new session. |
