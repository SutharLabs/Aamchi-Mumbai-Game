# GTA gameplay reference and Ranwar implementation

Desk review on **22 November 2026**. GTA V Story Mode and GTA Online serve different purposes. In Story Mode, missions can become available through character-specific progress, time, location and phone contacts. GTA Online uses cash for purchases and reputation for unlocks, with some one-time completion bonuses alongside its repeatable jobs. Ranwar uses a persistent story contact and an Indian rupee payout tied to one completed job. ### On-foot controls, camera and encounters

**Research.** Rockstar documents independent controls for walking and driving, selectable camera perspectives, aim settings and a separate controls page. Vice City's manual likewise distinguishes movement, vehicle actions and in-car radio. These references suggest that the player should have a clear interaction verb and that camera and control settings should match the active activity. Give the player a consistent interact action for talking, using doors, entering, mounting and exiting. Group keyboard, gamepad and future touch actions by walking, driving and menus. Extend the existing input when a concrete remapping or touch requirement calls for it. Enhanced Input mapping contexts are a candidate once device mappings or state transitions justify migration. An authored encounter in Phase 3 can use game-specific telegraphs, abstract hit and stamina states, a noncombat resolution and recovery. Scope additional encounter mechanics around what players can read on the lowest target display.
