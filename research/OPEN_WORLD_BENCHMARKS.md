# Open-world benchmarks for Aamchi Mumbai Game

Desk research checked **23 November 2026**. This appendix compares documented player-facing design and publicly documented sample architecture. Its recommendations apply to original Ranwar development. [Project brief](../docs/PROJECT_BRIEF.md) · [GTA V systems notes](GTA_GAMEPLAY_REFERENCES.md) · [Menu and HUD research](MENU_AND_HUD_RESEARCH.md) · [Phase evidence](../docs/PHASE_PROGRESS.md)

## Evidence from Rockstar

**Grand Theft Auto V.** Take-Two records the original 17 September 2013 PS3 and Xbox 360 release. Rockstar describes three playable perspectives in its authored story and freely changing perspective outside missions. The game help documents radar and wanted response. Support guidance shows why tasks need legible prerequisites. Story progress can depend on the current character, a contact or elapsed game time. The separate GTA Online guides describe a social and business economy, freemode and progression. [Take-Two release record](https://ir.take2games.com/static-files/14996949-bb1b-44a8-8089-efb0c54f9415) · [Rockstar on character structure](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o349k552517929/asked-answered-gtav.html) · [GTA V game help](https://dlassets-ssl.xboxlive.com/public/content/4f0a3089-ba2c-4f3d-9e38-102a41cbd885/GameManual/bffef18e-3f19-4190-a9b1-75402359b13b/en-SA/index.html) · [Story mission support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/2anfBi4NMr3QqtUMeum51b/what-to-do-if-no-further-missions-are-showing-up-in-gtav-story-mode) · [GTA Online guides](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-online/guides/)

**Grand Theft Auto VI.** Rockstar announced worldwide availability on **19 November 2026** for **PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S**. Take-Two's 9 November results anticipated that date. Rockstar describes Jason and Lucia, modern Vice City and surrounding Leonida locations, with an original score and in-game radio. The August Extended Look is publisher-selected in-game PS5 footage. Use it to study visible composition, density and the identity of different places. The sources verify the stated launch platforms, controls and settings. The technical implementation, actual population limits, future native platforms and online rollout schedule remain unverified in this study. [Launch notice](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39akk424a9oo53/grand-theft-auto-vi-now-available) · [Take-Two results](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4) · [Place profiles](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida/leonida-keys) · [Extended Look and capture method](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/4k138k8okkk483/grand-theft-auto-vi-an-extended-look-now-playing) · [In-game radio](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o3982oa93a23k4/the-music-of-grand-theft-auto-vi-in-game-radio-stations)

**GTA VI interface.** Rockstar's 18 November support article confirms pause-accessible settings, action-specific input choices, separate camera and audio controls, HUD and prompt options, UI scaling, subtitle and caption controls, and default restoration. It gives both Aamchi Mumbai versions a useful precedent for separating movement, vehicle and dialogue options by player intent. ## Design benchmarks by system

The applications below are design inferences. Published documentation establishes the benchmark behavior. Epic's City Sample establishes how the sample works in Unreal.

| System | Documented benchmark | Original Ranwar application and proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World identity and exploration | GTA VI's place profiles distinguish locations by characters and roles. Nintendo's Tears of the Kingdom guide shows early wayfinding and a recoverable adventure-log path. [Rockstar places](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida/leonida-keys) · [Nintendo journey guide](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/zelda/totk/guide/en/index.html) | Give Hill Road, Nagrana Lane and the Waroda approach visible landmarks, distinct widths and uses, and signs readable on foot and in vehicles. Prove a new player can find the bus stop and return with the live map off. Base streets on dated references and author the shop stories, art and details. |
| Navigation and streaming seams | Rockstar's map guidance describes place discovery and waypoints. Godot's HLOD and Unreal's World Partition document distinct ways to allocate detail by range. [Rockstar map guide](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-online/guides/7772) · [Godot visibility ranges](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/3d/visibility_ranges.html) · [Epic World Partition](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/world-partition-in-unreal-engine) | Derive the pause map, live map and route access from the same dated geometry. Sign authored closures. Keep route-critical assets, navigation and collision ready before the player arrives. Test a streaming seam with an active objective, both on foot and in the same occupied vehicle. |
| Missions and checkpoints | GTA V story support documents prerequisite-driven mission starts. GTA Online's heist update shows longer jobs composed from preparation and finale stages. [GTA V story mission support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/2anfBi4NMr3QqtUMeum51b/what-to-do-if-no-further-missions-are-showing-up-in-gtav-story-mode) · [Rockstar Online update](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/6hBJdQCmQ3YR7nqHIq9AGS/gtav-title-update-1-21-ps3-xbox-360-1-07-ps4-xbox-one) | Finish one original 10–15 minute job with an arrival, one transport or contact decision, handoff and aftermath. Make the objective, prerequisite, retry point and payout understandable. Try both intended routes, mission failure and abandonment, then confirm the correct next checkpoint from an app restart. |
| Pedestrians and traffic | Epic's sample generates crowd and traffic lanes from world data. Mass Gameplay exposes spawning, representation LOD and state tooling. [City Sample](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/city-sample-project-unreal-engine-demonstration?lang=en-US) · [Mass Gameplay](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/overview-of-mass-gameplay-in-unreal-engine) | Seed activity by road direction, places, and time. Nearby agents yield and make visible contact. Distant actors can update less often. Keep mission actors, boardable vehicles and immediate obstacles fully active until a safe transition. Replay a fixed seed at the BEST stop, a crossing and the lane mouth. |
| Walking and vehicles | GTA V documents vehicle-context inputs. Forza's documented opening introduces driving, choice of vehicle and one short story. Epic's sample documents tunable mass, gearing and center of mass. [Rockstar vehicle controls](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4b6n5wYVseQhdCo2bNpVY5/how-to-change-vehicle-controls-in-grand-theft-auto-v) · [Forza opening](https://support.forza.net/hc/en-us/articles/7892196752787-Hot-Wheels-Quick-Start-Guide) · [City Sample vehicles](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/city-sample-project-unreal-engine-demonstration?lang=en-US) | Give each family its own width, turning and stopping profile. Use the same curb, grade and narrow-lane cases for all four. Show the right-seat car driver and centered handlebar riders where appropriate. Verify walking-to-driving control transfer, parked clearance and safe exits. |
| Street interaction and relationships | Ubisoft describes interactions that affect surrounding actors and neighborhoods. Rockstar's Red Dead Redemption 2 Honor explainer describes persistent benefits and consequences from decisions. [Watch Dogs 2](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/games/watch-dogs-2) · [Rockstar Honor](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak14o88389271k/Honor-in-Red-Dead-Redemption-2) | Use stable verbs and visible contact points for talking, borrowing, entering and collecting. Let relationships create one useful change over a small cast. Exercise interaction priority with a door, NPC and vehicle close together. |
| Law, injury and recovery | GTA V game help documents detection and wanted feedback. CD PROJEKT RED's Update 2.0 documents an overhauled police response for Cyberpunk 2077. [GTA V game help](https://dlassets-ssl.xboxlive.com/public/content/4f0a3089-ba2c-4f3d-9e38-102a41cbd885/GameManual/bffef18e-3f19-4190-a9b1-75402359b13b/en-SA/index.html) · [Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.0](https://www.cyberpunk.net/en/news/49060/update-2-0) | Keep a compact fictional alert with a visible cause, readable patrol response and clear resolution. Check a staged scene under different encounter states. Give the clinic, technical recovery and mission retry separate cues and outcomes. |
| Economy and progression | GTA Online publishes careers, starter investments and jobs as a long-term progression structure. [Rockstar Online Career Builder](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/1UfYjMN0NnpJcoC5DLZkf4/gta-online-career-builder-overview) · [Freemode guide](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-online/guides/7772) | Link the existing rupee economy to one job, receipt, meaningful spend and next contact. Record the payout and mission transition together. Ensure retry, menu exit and reload preserve one-time rewards. |
| UX, subtitles and accessibility | GTA VI documents prompt, navigation, interface scaling and caption settings. Forza documents first-start access and independent dialogue levels. Microsoft publishes focus and readability guidance. [GTA VI settings](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4jUHNdP22SmaKSF8DWqhXC/how-to-change-grand-theft-auto-vi-settings) · [Forza accessibility](https://support.forza.net/hc/en-us/articles/46523995129747-Forza-Horizon-5-Accessibility-Support) · [Xbox guidelines](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/guidelines) | Keep matching destinations and settings behavior. Reserve distinct screen space for prompts, objectives and speech. Test useful options at title and pause, legible captions over bright streets, and actual keyboard, gamepad and Android touch. |
| Sound, dialogue and music | Rockstar publishes score and in-game radio as separate layers. Epic's sample drives street ambience and engines from world and vehicle state. [Rockstar radio](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o3982oa93a23k4/the-music-of-grand-theft-auto-vi-in-game-radio-stations) · [Epic City Sample soundscape](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/city-sample-project-unreal-engine-demonstration?lang=en-US) | Differentiate crossings, interiors, vehicles and cast through playing conditions and positions. Keep dialogue audible above traffic. Use original scripts and document rights for voices, recordings and compositions. Test overlapping voices, pause, mute, driving transfer and mobile audio-focus loss. |
| Art and quality profiles | Rockstar identifies the VI showing as captured PS5 imagery. Epic documents distinct mobile rendering paths and per-device profiles. [Rockstar capture method](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/4k138k8okkk483/grand-theft-auto-vi-an-extended-look-now-playing) · [Epic mobile profiles](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/customizing-device-profiles-and-scalability-in-unreal-engine-projects-for-android) | Spend detail on the visible streets, façade relief, surface transitions, contact shadows and rider poses. Preserve the same clear route and gameplay options at Low. Add optional lighting and density in measured profiles. Compare matched cameras and time a sustained route before raising the cost. |
| Saves and lifecycle | Rockstar documents one-time Story Mode migration and separate saves for different generations. [GTA V save migration](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4qpoUBQQGvZO3p1MXhHMA9/migrating-your-gtav-story-mode-save-game-from-playstation-4-or-xbox-one-to-playstation-5-or-xbox-series-x-s) | Keep recoverable per-profile checkpoints. Verify fresh saves, old versions, interrupted writes and retry or duplicate transition cases. Match gameplay facts and user-facing behavior across engines and document their save formats. Test a native Android app interruption as well as a Windows process restart. |
| Playtests, updates and live operations | Steamworks separates test branches and the public default build. Rockstar's product and support pages document separate editions and continuing GTA Online feature/balance updates. [Steamworks branches](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/application/branches) · [Rockstar Online improvements](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/75k42ak2422o4o/experience-improvements-coming-to-gta-online) | Treat reproducible packages, versions, notices, safe updates and rollback as early work. Test offline play, install integrity and an older saved game before publication. Ongoing multiplayer operations, economies and storefront entitlements belong to a separate scope decision. |

## Vertical slice before expansion

1. **Finish and observe a coherent route.** A fresh player finds a recognizable destination, makes one walking or driving decision, completes or retries a mission and understands the receipt and next contact. Restart the process and verify the same facts. The short Ranwar Parcel Run supplies a repeatable instrumentation route while the representative job is in development.
2. **Measure genuinely different costs.** Record median, p95 and p99 frame times, stalls, CPU and GPU time, world residency, crowded-junction actor count, draw calls and memory for an exact build and preset. Use cold boot and sustained repeated walk/drive cases. Test a camera cut, narrow lane, active interior and a busy junction before increasing the district size. [Epic timing](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/timing-insights-in-unreal-engine) · [Epic memory](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/memory-insights-in-unreal-engine) · [Godot 3D optimization](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/performance/optimizing_3d_performance.html)
3. **Use the intended physical hardware.** Keep 720p/30 FPS Low as the provisional Windows target. Record actual memory, renderer and frame pacing on the 8 GB integrated-GPU Windows 10 class and selected Redmi Note 9 Pro Max. Capture Android version, RAM, touch, interruption and heat during a repeated route. Keep the development Iris Xe render and simulated-touch runs as iteration evidence. [Project target record](../docs/PROJECT_RECORD.md) · [Android game optimization](https://developer.android.com/games/optimize) · [Unreal mobile performance](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/performance-guidelines-for-mobile-devices-in-unreal-engine)
4. **Test expansion in small units.** Review how a new block reads in the gameplay camera. Pair current Bandra references with measured clearances and asset rights. Use the Godot feedback loop before the comparable Unreal package, then retest progression, text and audio as density or lighting changes. Use original locations built from cleared map and reference inputs, original cast and fiction, and independently cleared music and assets.
