## Architectural evidence

- MHADA's 2019 Worli project document describes the older BDD buildings as ground plus three floors, 80 rooms per building and 160 sq ft tenements. These historical scale facts do not establish a complete as-built plan. [MHADA project document](https://www.mhada.gov.in/sites/default/files/PMC-Agreement-for-BDD-Redevelopment-Project-26_2_2019-Worli.pdf)
- The Chembur heritage report documents Kharwa Chawl's low-rise, two-row/courtyard arrangement and shared washing/service facilities. Treat this as a distinct study from multistorey gallery housing. [MMR-HCS Chembur report](https://mmrhcs.org.in/images/documents/projects/chembur/03-final-stage-5-revised-report-15-12-11.pdf)
- MMR-HCS's G-South proformas provide further BDD setting/facade/stair references; the large PDF exceeded the research browser's fetch limit, so obtain/view the relevant pages before detailed matching. [G-South proformas](https://www.mmrhcs.org.in/images/documents/projects/03-Gs3-Proformas.pdf)
- MHADA's 2025 Worli release records rehabilitation allocation. Use it as dated redevelopment evidence, then verify the current parcel/form; older BDD descriptions and models may show demolished or altered conditions, and Worli is not Ranwar. [Redevelopment release](https://www.mhada.gov.in/sites/default/files/Press_release_Worli_BDD_Chawl_allotment_programme_of_556_rehab_tenements-dtd-29-08-2025.pdf)

Reference photographs have their own rights. The procedural studies below use geometric concepts and documented typology, without importing those photographs or external models.

## Queued original studies

The first pass makes a reusable frontage bay, switchback stair, repeated-gallery housing assembly, separate baithi courtyard assembly and service props. The gallery study uses four bays and four levels as a small design experiment, not a replica of an 80-room BDD building.

Working dimensions such as 3.0 m bay width, 5.0 m room depth, 1.4 m gallery width and 2.8 m floor height are explicitly **estimated design parameters**, not surveyed measurements. Compare actual references before accepting them. Door/window apertures, shared circulation, connected stairs and open courtyard access are review priorities.

Each job exports editable geometry, GLB, front/rear/side/game-angle PNGs, triangle/material/bounds statistics and a reference/assumption report. Technical export success leaves current local architectural accuracy, playability, collision, touch-camera interaction and populated-scene Windows/Android performance pending.
