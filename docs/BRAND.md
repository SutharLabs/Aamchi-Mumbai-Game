# Brand guide — Aamchi Mumbai Game

## Selected game title

**Aamchi Mumbai Game**

Selected on 22 November 2026. The 20 November GitHub baseline and archive used the former title, **Mumbai: Gully Heat**.

## Proposed tagline

**Mumbai never slows down.**

## Brand idea

A fast, colorful, street-level open-world adventure based on real Mumbai. The identity centers on neighborhood rivalries, narrow-lane chases, local ambition, and Mumbai street life. Real buildings, landmarks, street relationships, and vehicle models establish the setting, with stylized geometry for modest hardware. Story, cast, missions, and branding are original. Monsoon rain, sea air, and late-night light support the city's observed character.

The user selected **present-day Mumbai only** on **22 November 2026**, retaining the Ranwar/Bandra West pocket and original fictional cast/story. Contemporary signs, clothing, vehicles and streetscape details use dated references and commercially usable inputs. Keep interfaces readable for Windows 10 and Android 12. The minimum Android phone remains open.

## Startup identity

The title screen pairs **आमची मुंबई** with **Our Mumbai**. The background is an original generated illustration of a fictional Ranwar-inspired lane after rain, using the neighborhood's narrow frontages, tiled roofs, verandahs and street-facing windows. Set the title and controls in native UI text over the dark side of the composition. [Mumbai name and street research](../research/STARTUP_AND_CREDITS.md)

The playtest mark combines a gold M with the front of a black-and-yellow auto-rickshaw. The PNG is used in the menu, project and boot splash. The multi-size ICO provides the Windows file and taskbar icon. Preserve the dark rounded field and the large M at small sizes. [Playtest mark](../prototype/startup/art/amg-mark.png)

## Voice

- Energetic, streetwise, observant, and playful.
- Mumbai is alive and specific; never reduce it to generic chaos or a tourist postcard.
- Humor comes from characters and situations, not from mocking local communities.
- Use concise, memorable language. Avoid imitating dialogue, slogans, or writing from existing games.

## Visual identity

- **Primary palette:** monsoon indigo, wet-asphalt charcoal, electric cyan, and warm taxi amber.
- **Accent:** vivid coral or hot pink used sparingly for nightlife and mission emphasis.
- **Typography:** bold condensed display lettering for the title; clean, highly legible sans serif for interface and mission information.
- **Texture:** rain sheen, halftone grain, painted sign surfaces, sun-faded print, and restrained 2D poster wear.
- **Shape language:** angled road markings, local-train route geometry, rain arcs, and layered coastal horizons.
- **Title lockup:** use bold readable type for Aamchi Mumbai Game. Pair it with the standalone rickshaw mark and the native Marathi title in the startup view.

## Logo and key art rules

- Keep the wordmark readable at game-library thumbnail size.
- Build a standalone silhouette that works in one color before adding effects.
- Do not use Rockstar, Grand Theft Auto, Vice City, or existing game typefaces, logos, maps, characters, vehicles, missions, or signature artwork.
- Avoid defaulting to a palm-tree / Miami skyline composition. Use Mumbai-inspired street details and coastal geometry instead.
- Base street signs, vehicle body shapes, and local paint/service categories on dated Mumbai references. Record exact logo, advertising, photograph, and source-model reuse terms separately; do not assume a public reference image is a shipping texture.
- Preserve neighborhood-specific architecture and transport geography. Follow `../research/MUMBAI_REAL_WORLD_REFERENCES.md`; classic-game style is not a substitute for actual local reference.

## UI and in-game application

- Use amber for navigation, cyan for interaction, and coral for urgent mission states; keep colors consistent.
- Keep HUD shapes flat and high-contrast for low-spec rendering and small screens.
- Let rain and neon enrich backgrounds, never reduce text readability.
- District labels, mission cards, map legends, and save menus should share the same angular route-line motif.

## Short pitch

A stylized open-world action and driving game where neighborhood rivalries, narrow-lane chases, and local ambition unfold among recognizable real-Mumbai streets, buildings, landmarks, and vehicles.

## Status

Aamchi Mumbai Game is the selected title. The startup art and rickshaw mark are in the Phase 1 Windows build. The tagline and broader palette remain proposals.
