# Unreal open-world feature audit and acceptance plan

24 November 2026. Aamchi Mumbai Game targets an original, offline third-person story in a compact, present-day Ranwar district. The first release still targets Windows 10 on an 8 GB integrated-GPU PC and Android 12 on the Redmi Note 9 Pro Max (India). The development specification owns those targets and the phase tracker owns test results. This audit turns the visual and gameplay benchmarks into acceptance checks for the Unreal version. [Development specification](OPEN_WORLD_GAME_SPEC.md) · [Phase tracker](PHASE_PROGRESS.md) · [Benchmark research](../research/OPEN_WORLD_BENCHMARKS.md)

## Reference observations

- **GTA V.** Rockstar documents increased draw distance, finer surface detail, denser traffic, wildlife and improved weather and damage in the upgraded game. It also explains what players can check when story missions stop appearing. Aamchi can use those player-facing ideas at district scale. Give a route a readable distance, warn the player before it becomes unavailable, and make a collision or story prerequisite change something the player can see or hear. [Rockstar’s GTA V world overview](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o349k552525a57/Grand-Theft-Auto-V-Coming-this-Fall-to-PlayStation-4-Xbox) · [Rockstar’s story progression support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/2anfBi4NMr3QqtUMeum51b/what-to-do-if-no-further-missions-are-showing-up-in-gtav-story-mode)
- **GTA VI.** In Rockstar’s *Vice City 27*, a cool dusk sky, warm street pools and distance haze separate traffic, buildings and water. *Vice City 14* and *19* show distinct surface qualities for cloth, skin and car upholstery up close. The Ranwar D3D11 Kavita handoff already has contact shadows, a weathered wall and readable dialogue. Its unbroken window rows, bare road edges and empty horizon expose the next art priorities. Start at gameplay distance with recessed openings, a few occupied street edges and a recognizable hazy background. Spend close-up character detail around the shop encounters where it will be seen. These published stills guide the visual review. Performance claims come from the playable Ranwar route. [Rockstar’s official GTA VI screenshots](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media/screenshots) · [Ranwar’s reviewed handoff, E-60](PHASE_PROGRESS.md#evidence-already-completed)
- **Settings and transitions.** Rockstar documents separate control schemes by context and settings for the HUD, camera, dialogue captions and interface scale. The 23 November GTA VI update identifies real interaction failures worth testing in Aamchi, including weather persisting too long, autosaves, story calls, wanted-state clearance and handoffs. Compare the flow and test the transitions. Create Mumbai’s own wording, visual identity, locations, cast, story and sound. [GTA VI settings](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4jUHNdP22SmaKSF8DWqhXC/how-to-change-grand-theft-auto-vi-settings) · [GTA VI launch update](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/sN5pEdFYZdlvijKXd6xpJ/grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-updates-ps5-xbox-series-x-or-s)
- **Unreal implementation references.** Epic’s City Sample demonstrates a dynamic city, audio and traffic, with a recommended 64 GB system and DX12 graphics. Ranwar can borrow the principle of budgeting nearer and farther activity separately while using its DX11 baseline. Epic’s Game Animation Sample shows how starts, turns, stops and foot planting can be handled. Test those motions on Omkar and the existing rig before evaluating motion matching. Epic’s mobile renderer documentation supports a separate Android lighting and effects budget. [City Sample](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/city-sample-project-unreal-engine-demonstration?lang=en-US) · [Game Animation Sample](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/game-animation-sample-project-in-unreal-engine?lang=en-US) · [Mobile rendering features](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/mobile-rendering-features-in-unreal-engine)

- **Mumbai street lighting.** The BMC fixture reference includes compact column-mounted LED luminaires with warm, neutral and cool white options and directional distributions. Ranwar uses this as a fixture reference and places the poles by walking routes, road edges and the arrival stop. Individual real-world pole positions require a field or imagery survey. [BMC fixture reference](https://www.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/Tenders/M%26E/ME_8000104879_020326.pdf)

## Implementation order and exit checks

1. **Finish the atmosphere pass on the existing route.** Reuse the sun, skylight, sky atmosphere, inexpensive height fog, clock and three existing camera views. Use the same gameplay camera at the BEST stop, Nagrana and a shop threshold. Prove daytime, dusk and dark gameplay in the packaged Low profile before raising geometry or lighting cost. For a follow-up monsoon pass, seed a clear-to-overcast-to-light-rain sequence, fade nearby rain and surface wetness together, and vary the rain sound between an open lane and shelter. Run an active mission across the transition, pause and reload.
2. **Prove the ordinary-world reactions.** Verify four-family vehicle handling and seated drivers, then one stopped traffic transfer, one pedestrian crossing and one bounded police or injury outcome. Test a recoverable crash while a job is active. Persist and show the player-facing outcome before adding wider dispatch, heavier physics or animal behavior.
3. **Measure a complete first play.** Use the current arrival, parcel job, ledger lead and repeatable work to make one coherent 10–15 minute route. On the 8 GB Windows 10 target, use 1280 × 720 Low and record warm p95 against 33.3 ms, repeated stalls above 100 ms, process working set against the provisional 2 GB target and shared GPU memory. On the named phone, record a repeated 15-minute native-touch lap, device/OS, frame pacing, memory, thermals and interruption. Keep the route, population seed, settings and before/after cameras fixed. [Project evidence protocol](OPEN_WORLD_GAME_SPEC.md#performance-and-evidence-protocol)
4. **Use the slice to size the release.** Close repeatability, first-play, recovery and both hardware gates. Then expand resident and mission activity within Ranwar, finish its original story, and use long-session and clean-install testing for release readiness. Advance a linked district, elaborate transit or expensive render features after evidence shows what the next area actually needs.
