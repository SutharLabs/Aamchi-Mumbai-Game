## Reference packet before detailed modeling

For each accepted production brief, complete one reference record with its asset ID and intended neighborhood/mission role. Cross-link it to the geometry record instead of treating a model's title as proof of identity.

1. **Place and year:** define the selected Bandra West pocket's boundary and collect dated subject references within the settled 1990-1995 year-band. Record coordinates or map anchor/object ID, source date, actual frontage/approach orientation and confidence. Keep Bandra, Dadar, Worli, Girgaon and Colaba in separate location groups. Record every compression or relocation as an authored change. Queued chawls remain typology studies until a local period packet supports placement.
2. **Views and scale:** obtain front, side and rear or a documented reason why a view is missing; add roof/plan context for buildings and rider/driver contact views for vehicles. Use authorized photos and original observations where possible. Record each dimension as measured, OEM-published, map-derived or estimated, with units and source. Unknown sides remain unknown rather than AI-inferred survey facts.
3. **Subject version:** record the exact vehicle generation, trim/body type and year; for a building record its selected historical state. The 2019 BDD description, 2025 redevelopment notice and 2016 model leads cannot establish a 1990-1995 building state. A 2024 Bandra station photo is a later comparator, requiring dated evidence for the intended period. Current OEM data and landmark views stay on hold for period shipping until period fit is demonstrated.
4. **Construction and identity:** capture the recognition cues to preserve at game-camera distance. For a chawl, this includes dwelling/gallery/stair/courtyard relationships and usable openings. For a nauvari variant it includes divided lower drape, tuck/pleat construction and pallu route, examined from the rear as well as front. Historical cultural references inform construction; neither they nor modern street references establish selected-era clothing prevalence.
5. **Rights per layer:** track map data, photos, input images, geometry, materials, logos/sign artwork, tool/model license and generated outputs separately. An OEM or museum web page is evidence of a subject; it is not an automatically cleared texture or image-to-3D input. Preserve creator, actual license/version, source URL, acquired filename/date/hash, required credit and modifications when something is acquired.
6. **Role and technical brief:** define camera distance, silhouette test, dimensions/tolerance, LOD intent, materials, attachment points, collision and vehicle role. Any image-to-3D attempt needs rights-cleared inputs and a recorded tool/model/version/terms snapshot; the current CPU-only procedural queue does not supply an evaluated image-to-3D service.

Current estimated chawl parameters are 3.0 m frontage bay, 5.0 m room depth, 1.4 m gallery and 2.8 m floor height. They are generator design values, not MHADA measurements. The four-bay/four-level study is not a replica of an 80-room BDD block. Resolve reference contradictions before polishing detail.

The image-to-3D hypothesis remains untested. Run a paired experiment on small props before using it to schedule vehicles, garments or a district. The current original procedural queue is a third route to compare where relevant; it is not an image-reconstruction benchmark. No model service, GPU pipeline or specific license has been accepted for image-to-3D yet.

Set the same human review budget and a finite attempt/compute cap for both routes before starting. A practical initial protocol is at most three generation/rework rounds per route, followed by a recorded accept/reject/blocked decision; an over-budget failure remains in the result. This is an experiment control, not a prediction of production time. Randomize route order across items where practical to reduce learning effects, and have the reviewer compare the same rendered views without being told the route where feasible.

Record the following for **every attempt**, including rejected outputs:

- Source record and acquired-input hashes; input preparation/photography/rights-check minutes; tool/model/version/terms/date and parameters; exact prompts, seeds and output hashes where available.
- Generation/reconstruction compute duration, queue/wait time, retries, failed calls and charged credits/currency; local/remote machine identity, thread/GPU use and metered compute cost if known. Record unknown costs as unknown, not zero.
- Separate human minutes for searching/acquiring a reusable asset, manual/procedural construction, retopology, cleanup, UVs, textures/materials, dimension/pivot fixes, collision/LOD, import, review and all rework. Record Codex/agent tokens or actual usage cost where available, plus human coordination time. A subscription's marginal allocation is an explicit assumption, not free work.
- Final triangles, surfaces/materials, texture memory estimates and measured imported memory where available, collision shapes, visual defects and rights obligations. Record paired-scene performance deltas in both exported platform builds after the actual Windows/Android devices and engine are available.
- Gate outcomes, reviewer/date and why an attempt failed. Count time until acceptance; stopped/failed routes have no accepted-asset time. Show human hours, elapsed wall time and cash/compute separately before any optional combined-cost calculation using a stated hourly rate.

Report paired results per item and by prop class, then median/range only where the sample supports it. Do not divide all elapsed time by accepted assets while hiding failed attempts. Ten props cannot establish speed for drivable vehicles, deforming clothes or accurate buildings. Adopt G only for the categories where accepted quality, rights, human effort, total cost and engine cost justify it. If neither route passes, revise the brief or source packet and keep that result visible.

## Escalation and re-estimation rules

- **Rights/provenance conflict:** hold acquisition/adoption or generator-input use at A0; preserve the conflicting notices and choose another source or an original route. Do not label Free Standard, custom commercial terms or download availability as CC0/CC BY. Do not copy the disputed component while waiting for clarity.
- **Wrong place/year/type:** if a candidate is a different vehicle generation, generic house, unrelated drape or demolished/redeveloped state, resolve the reference brief or select another candidate before cleanup. Do not silently change the fixed Mumbai identity requirements to fit a convenient asset.
- **Cultural/architectural uncertainty:** seek specific dated reference/reviewer input and keep the study unapproved. Continue independent period-clothing research or generic technical work. A missing reviewer is not evidence that the current form is accurate or period-appropriate.
- **Repeated export/runtime failure:** after the queue's bounded attempts, retain failed logs and fix the smallest relevant spec/generator in a reviewed version. An occupied shared lock/window interruption is different from a failed mesh. Follow the runbook; do not delete checkpoints or disturb another workload.
- **Cleanup cost defeats reuse/generation:** when a route reaches the predeclared experiment/rework cap without passing its gate, record sunk effort and compare the manual/original route. Do not justify indefinite cleanup from the amount already spent.
- **Populated build misses budget:** identify asset-family/material/animation/collision/population deltas before adding detail; reduce the measured bottleneck and retest the same route on both platforms. Preserve the user-required four player-drivable families and simultaneous Windows/Android release; escalate schedule, variants and optional detail rather than silently substituting parked vehicles or deferring Android. No GPU-less remote result substitutes for either player-device test.
- **Review backlog grows:** pause new variants and assign agents to defects/provenance until H can accept work. Report completed technical jobs and accepted production assets as separate counts. Re-estimate at AP-20 from accepted throughput, including rejected attempts and integration time; continuous agents are not an automatic calendar multiplier.
