# Aamchi Mumbai Game development specification

Revision 1.0, 23 November 2026. This specification defines original player-facing behavior, technical contracts and acceptance checks. The roadmap owns scope and scheduling, the phase tracker owns completion evidence, and the benchmark appendix owns external evidence. Use these requirements for implementation in the existing game. [Project brief](PROJECT_BRIEF.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) · [Phase tracker](PHASE_PROGRESS.md) · [Open-world benchmarks](../research/OPEN_WORLD_BENCHMARKS.md)

## Product and release envelope

- **Experience.** An offline, third-person, single-player story in present-day Mumbai. Omkar arrives in Bandra West, learns the neighborhood through residents and paid work, and follows the print-shop ledger into original crime fiction. The player can continue a mission or roam, and can read their next objective without already knowing the district.
- **Geography.** Start in the compact Ranwar pocket around Hill Road, St Peter’s Road, Chapel and Waroda Road. Preserve the relationship between real streets and landmarks, and record authored widths, distances, fictional interiors and other changes. Grow through walkable and drivable connections when a populated district proves stable.
- **Visual target.** Grounded, locally recognizable street scenes in the normal gameplay camera. Prioritize silhouette, scale, material response, façade depth, believable contact and animation, and light and sound at the player’s position. Keep a low-cost visual profile and measure before raising density or effects. Use dated local field or permitted reference images and record actual provenance and authored details. [Reference checklist](RANWAR_REFERENCE_CHECKLIST.md) · [Existing realism work order](../research/RANWAR_REALISM_EXECUTION.md)
- **Platforms.** Windows 10 x64 with keyboard/mouse and gamepad and Android 12 with touch. The Windows minimum class is 8 GB RAM with integrated graphics. The named Android test phone is the Redmi Note 9 Pro Max (India), with the installed build and actual hardware variant to record. Keep both physical-device gates open until measured. Make a car, auto-rickshaw, motorcycle and scooter drivable.
- **First-release planning envelope.** One compact district, one protagonist, one story ending and post-story free roam. Six main missions, two or three repeatables and a 60–120 minute first playthrough are roadmap targets to re-estimate from the slice. Multiplayer, city-wide reproduction, air and water vehicles, and broader live services are later scope decisions.
- **Development ownership.** Iterate in Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility, then validate the matching gameplay contracts, shared assets and packaged Windows build in Unreal 5.8.4. Treat native Android export, input, frame pacing and sustained thermal behavior as separate engineering proof. Keep original code and engine-specific save formats in their existing projects. [Build workflow](BUILD_WORKFLOW.md)

## What the benchmarks change

GTA V's documented mission prerequisites, story progression, radar and vehicle-context controls motivate readable job availability, returning from free roam and clear consequences. Its officially captured footage helps assess visual composition and density. The Ranwar architecture and budgets come from tests in this project and engine documentation. ## First implementation work order

### AMG-P3-LEDGER · The Hill Road Ledger

This is an original continuation of the existing **Ranwar Parcel Run**, linked to work orders A–G in the earlier realism brief through shared locations, short voiced performances, readable destinations and serialized mission progress. The work is sized as one checkpointed mission increment within Phase 3. [Earlier realism brief](../research/RANWAR_REALISM_EXECUTION.md) · [Ranwar rules](RANWAR_GAME_RULES.md)

| Field | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Mission ID | `hill_road_ledger`. Name the mission **The Hill Road Ledger**. The active objective names the contact or destination, and the completion notice names the job and payment. |
| Prerequisite | Parcel Run completed. In Godot, log Mira’s ledger lead through the existing conversation. In Unreal, the saved Parcel Run ending already delivers Mira’s call. Compatible older completed Unreal parcels qualify. |
| Available | Show Asha’s destination at Hill Road Tea Room. Derive the interior location from the existing mapped storefront record for OSM way **360291657**. |
| Collect | On foot and within normal interaction reach, take Asha’s copy of the ledger. Save the **handoff** checkpoint before changing the visible objective. Play `asha-ledger-pickup` with a readable caption. |
| Travel | Recommend the shorter mapped route along Hill Road toward St Peter’s Road and the Chapel/Waroda approach. Keep the connected street network usable. The player can walk or drive the street portion and must leave any vehicle to interact. |
| Handoff | On foot and within normal reach, give the ledger copy to the grocer inside Chapel Corner Grocer, OSM way **357472313**. Save **completed** before awarding **₹400 once** and playing `grocer-ledger-handoff`. The match to Mira’s receipts gives the player a story outcome. |
| Pause and recovery | Load the last valid checkpoint on return from pause, profile or process restart. Keep one active objective if other supported activities are in progress. Follow existing safe recovery policy. Where pause offers mission abandonment, return to collection with no completion receipt and consistent prompts. |
| Compatibility | Keep the existing parcel, career, arrival and engine-local profiles valid. Preserve unrelated active jobs and payouts. Initialize the new mission from the established prerequisites and resolve payout from the committed completion flag. Preserve the last good checkpoint on a failed save. |
| Presentation | Use existing storefront collision, mapped anchors, objective markers, HUD, input and subtitle preferences. Introduce only mission contacts and cues needed for the job. |

## Performance and evidence protocol

Keep the saved map revision, exact package configuration, camera route and actor seed consistent when comparing pass and baseline. Separate an intentionally uncapped capture from the normal 30 FPS play target. Keep trigger locations playable during changeover between assets or simulation tiers.

| Target | Starting method and check |
| --- | --- |
| Windows minimum | Name the actual 8 GB integrated-GPU hardware, Windows 10 version and driver. On 1280 × 720 Low, start at 30 FPS. Check warm p95 frame time ≤33.3 ms, recurring stalls over 100 ms and a provisional ≤2 GB process working set. Capture system commit and shared GPU memory as well. |
| Android target | Record Android/MIUI, the Redmi hardware variant, renderer, render resolution and input. Begin a Low-profile 30 FPS experiment and run a repeated 15-minute route with native touch. Record p95, visible stalls, PSS or comparable memory, thermal behavior, app interruption and audio focus. Set the accepted memory and rendering budget from measurements on the phone. |
| Capture sequence | Observe a cold launch, three warm laps, a busy junction, a shop handoff, pause/resume, vehicle transfer and checkpoint/reload. Log p50/p95/p99, maximum and repeated stalls, draw calls, active actors, GPU/CPU timing where available, and distributions over time. |
| Visual QA | Use the same camera height, resolution, lighting and stage for before/after. Inspect the whole play view, character motion, wheels or feet, signs, slopes and light transitions. Compare normal movement and a held frame. |
| Scale-up rule | Start with current working population. Test one change to density, material cost or streaming at a time. Reduce optional effects and distant representation cost before reducing gameplay readability or route availability. Adopt a heavier engine feature only after a measured comparison. |

## Development order after the Ledger increment

1. **Finish Phase 3.** Integrate one representative narrative encounter and consequence, build a measured route from a fresh profile, and verify checkpoint, rewards, four-family vehicle handling, both native platforms and actual newcomer play. The existing short missions and 200 m art route are reusable fixtures. Gate G3 controls campaign expansion.
2. **Produce content in Phase 4.** Plan each job with stable ID, locations, required world state, objective sequence, save and recovery behavior, voiced cues, cost and evidence. Make original content with the same reusable systems. Review the game-wide ending and post-ending free-roam state before expanding side activities.
3. **Use Phase 5 for alpha/beta acceptance.** Exercise long sessions, device heat, audio loss, updates, profile recovery, full campaigns and the heaviest intended scene. Triage by player impact and fix blockers before entering release preparation.
4. **Use Phase 6 for release readiness.** Produce the approved product artifacts for both targets, verify clean installs and save migration, review notices and screenshots, and confirm rollback.

For each work order, read the owning files, choose affected requirement and test IDs, capture a reproducible baseline, implement the smallest end-to-end change in Godot, and check it from the ordinary gameplay entry. Then port the contract to Unreal and verify the packaged behavior. Attach evidence to the phase tracker, recording build, device, controls and any remaining gates. Release publishing follows the repository’s established approval and Ubuntu workflow.
