# Ranwar frontage and chawl review

Desk review dated **22 November 2026**. The twelve authored frontages are candidates for the present-day Ranwar play area. Individual parcel placement, current street access and any local chawl placement await field evidence. Use the existing driving loop and Nagrana Lane for this review. The extracted road geometry provides the location baseline, and the engine layout records authored widths, setbacks and other gameplay changes. ## Image-guided streetscape pilot

The playable pilot adapts the timber gallery, diamond-pattern grilles and exterior return stair from Chandan Nandrajog's single **28 October 2006** Chapel Road photograph. The mesh uses original colour, scale, roofing and wall details within an existing authored Waroda lot, about **57 m from the Nagrana Lane mouth**. The historic image establishes an architectural form. Its date and viewpoint leave current site condition, depth and the original parcel unverified. The photograph is credited in the game. [Photograph and recorded capture date](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bandra_old_house.jpg) · [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/)

The Commons files near the Nagrana entrance also include a **26 August 2018** wide rooftop photograph from an upper floor of Holy Family Hospital. It records a nearby view from one position. A current, overlapping street-level set for the selected frontage remains to be acquired. [Frederick Noronha's geotagged photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bandra,_a_bird%27s_eye_view.jpg)

Google's Street View rules prohibit taking imagery out of the embedded viewer and creating data by extracting or tracing it. The Maps Platform terms also restrict creating content from Google Maps data. Licensed source photography and the existing OpenStreetMap route provide the production inputs for this game. [Google Geo Guidelines](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/) · [Google Maps Platform terms, §3.2.3](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms)

For multi-view reconstruction, collect a dated sequence of rights-cleared photos around one selected façade and the adjacent lane. Photograph the front and both visible corners from multiple positions, with the same feature in at least three overlapping images and consistent lens settings and lighting. Record a scale measurement and the location for alignment to the map. Owner-approved originals are suitable inputs. Mapillary's CC BY-SA imagery is a candidate when the actual sequence, attribution, capture date and applicable terms can be verified. Check the sparse reconstruction before baking and simplifying a mesh for the game's geometry budget. [COLMAP capture guidance](https://colmap.github.io/tutorial) · [Mapillary imagery licence](https://help.mapillary.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001770409-CC-BY-SA-license-for-open-data)

### Current photo searches and registered building pilot

On **23 November 2026**, a Wikimedia Commons API geosearch returned ten geotagged files within 220 m of Nagrana Lane and four within 210 m of St Peter’s. Among the results were the August 2018 rooftop views from Holy Family Hospital and a September 2018 St Peter’s file. The results cover isolated or elevated views, leaving the current overlapping ground-level set pending. The MapComplete and OpenStreetMap France Panoramax catalogues returned zero items for the Ranwar driving-loop bounds. KartaView returned zero items from a 120 m search around St Peter’s. Coverage from the broader KartaView, federated Panoramax and Mapillary searches remains unverified. [Commons geosearch](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Geosearch) · [St Peter’s Commons file and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/page/72967655) · [Panoramax catalogue API](https://docs.panoramax.fr/federated-catalog/overview/) · [KartaView photography API](https://kartaview.org/doc/photos)

The playable map now includes **Meena at 82 Hill Road**, on the opposite side of Hill Road from the Nagrana Lane junction. Its ten ordered footprint vertices and tagged 15 levels come from the preserved OSM extract. The engine projects them into the same metre coordinates as the street. The exterior uses original materials and a nominal 3.05 m per floor. The facade rhythm and modeled height of 45.75 m remain estimates for field comparison. A background lot is suppressed where its box would overlap the mapped structure. [OSM building footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/255362104) · [Engine site controls](../prototype/world/layout.json) · [OpenStreetMap data offer and attribution](../assets/maps/reference/bandra-ranwar-modern-osm-2026-11-20/ATTRIBUTION.md)

## Field review

| Stop | Record or compare |
| --- | --- |
| Hill Road from A to F | Check representative shop bays, shutter and canopy depth, walkable width, curb changes and setbacks. Compare with the eight authored Hill Road lots. |
| Western Waroda near E and the two Nagrana mouths, E and F | Check the four nearby frontage lots, roof and plinth proportions, frontage sequence, gates, any steps or bollards, and the walking connection through to Hill Road. |
| D and C, via Waroda and Chapel | Record signs by approach and vehicle class, one-way arrows and turn restrictions. Check the change from residential frontages to shopfronts and the width where pedestrians and vehicles pass. |
| St Peter's approach between F and B | Compare gate, compound wall, forecourt and steps from the sidewalk with the playable church approach. |
| A verified local chawl candidate | Record street and confirmed parcel, floor count, door rhythm, shared gallery or courtyard, stair connections and service areas where access permits. Mark dimensions as measured or estimated. |

For each finding record the **observation date, location or source ID, facing direction, visible feature, rights status and corresponding game lot or route change**. Use notes or sketches where photography is unwelcome. The linked material is for reference. Keep game art original and record per-file permission before using a photograph, mural, sign or other outside asset. [Asset acceptance criteria](ASSET_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md#visual-direction-and-review)
